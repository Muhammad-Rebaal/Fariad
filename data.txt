Assuming you are looking to report the severe road collapse shown in "road.jpeg" on anthign street, you will need to contact the relevant municipal authorities in Malir. Because jurisdiction in Malir is split between regular municipal zones and military cantonment limits, you must verify your exact boundary to determine whether the Karachi Metropolitan Corporation (KMC) or Cantonment Board Malir is responsible.

Karachi Metropolitan Corporation (KMC)
If your street is in a non-cantonment area of Malir, KMC (and the delegated District Municipal Corporation) is responsible for road infrastructure and repairs.

Phone Helpline: Call 1339, which is KMC's dedicated line where an operator will log and route your complaint. You can also use the Sindh-wide Local Government Department helpline at 1093.

Online CCIS Portal: Visit complaint.kmc.gos.pk/ccis and select "Log New Complaint". You will need to select your town/UC or nearest landmark to proceed. Do not use the "contact us" page on the main KMC website, as it is currently running placeholder content and will not process your request
