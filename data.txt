Road damage in Block 6, Gulshan-e-Iqbal falls under the jurisdiction of the Karachi Metropolitan Corporation (KMC). You can file a formal municipal complaint using the specific channels they actively monitor.

Filing a Complaint with KMC

Phone Helpline: Call the dedicated KMC line at 1339. The operator will log and route the complaint. Alternatively, the 24/7 Sindh-wide local government complaint line is 1093.

Online Portal: Go directly to the CCIS system at complaint.kmc.gos.pk/ccis and select "Log New Complaint".
